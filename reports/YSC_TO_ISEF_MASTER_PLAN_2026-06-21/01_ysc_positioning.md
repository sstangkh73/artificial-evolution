# YSC Positioning

Prepared: `2026-06-21`

## 1. Bottom-line positioning

The project should be positioned for `YSC` as:

`A student-built artificial-life research platform that already demonstrates early ecological learning phenomena and has uncovered the real structural barriers to stable endogenous evolution.`

That is a much stronger and safer frame than:

- `we solved open-ended evolution`
- `we already built artificial civilization`
- `we proved agents truly understand farming`

## 2. Why this positioning works for YSC

For `YSC`, strong projects usually win by being:

- original
- technically deep
- experimentally honest
- understandable

This project already has those ingredients if presented correctly:

- the system appears substantially self-built
- the world model is richer than a toy agent demo
- the reports include falsification and negative results
- there are concrete measurable findings, not just ideas

## 3. Best current YSC thesis

Recommended thesis for the current stage:

`In a resource-constrained artificial world, simple agents can develop reward-place learning and produce real ecological feedback loops, but the transition to stable multi-generation evolution is blocked by specific structural constraints.`

This thesis helps because it:

- keeps the claims inside the evidence
- lets positive and negative results support the same story
- makes the work sound like science, not overhyped simulation art

## 4. What to claim confidently at YSC

Based on the current evidence set, the safest strong claims are:

- the world supports a real `seed -> plant -> fruit -> consumed` ecological loop in tuned conditions
- agents return to rewarding locations far above chance
- agent-moved seeds can causally re-enter the plant cycle and later become food
- the project identified non-obvious structural barriers preventing stable reproductive evolution

## 5. What not to overclaim at YSC

Avoid claiming:

- true open-ended evolution has already been achieved
- stable long-run self-sustaining evolution is already solved
- agents intentionally farm in the human sense
- patches are definitively agent-owned managed farms
- all multi-seed evidence is already fully clean unless the seed plumbing audit is done

## 6. The strongest competition angle

The strongest angle is not just:

`I built a cool simulation.`

It is:

`I built a scientific system, tested it, found early success, then discovered the hidden reasons it still fails to cross into robust evolution.`

That is the kind of story judges remember because it shows:

- engineering skill
- research thinking
- honesty
- growth beyond a demo

## 7. Recommended one-sentence introduction

Suggested opening line for `YSC`:

`This project studies what conditions are necessary for artificial agents in a realistic resource-limited world to move from short-term survival behavior into stable multi-generation evolution.`

## 8. Recommended visual emphasis

For `YSC`, the visual story should prioritize:

- one clean world diagram
- one results panel for reward-place learning
- one results panel for seed-causality / ecological feedback
- one barrier panel showing why stable evolution still fails

That sequence tells a stronger scientific story than showing too many subsystems.
