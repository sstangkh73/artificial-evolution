# YSC to ISEF Roadmap

Prepared: `2026-06-21`

## 1. Core idea

`YSC` and `ISEF` should not be treated as two unrelated projects.

The strongest path is:

- use `YSC` to prove the project is original, rigorous, and alive
- use the period after `YSC` to convert the best current barrier story into an `ISEF`-level flagship result

## 2. Stage map

### Stage 1: YSC-ready project

Required outcome:

- a clear and defensible research story
- strong evidence for early learning and ecological coupling
- honest barrier analysis for why stable evolution is still hard

At this stage the project wins by being sharp, credible, and memorable.

### Stage 2: YSC-to-ISEF transition work

Required outcome:

- fix the highest-importance methodological weaknesses
- run the transition experiments that most directly test stable endogenous evolution

This is the technical bridge stage.

### Stage 3: ISEF-ready flagship

Required outcome:

- either a stable evolving regime with strong evidence
- or a rigorous structural negative-results thesis strong enough to stand alone internationally

## 3. Exact bridge from YSC to ISEF

### Bridge item 1: replication integrity

Problem:
past results include a known risk that some multi-seed runs were not true independent replications.

Action:

- audit all major RNG pathways
- tie behavior-critical randomness to the official seed model
- document the replication protocol

Why it matters:

- without this, `ISEF`-level claims are vulnerable even if the phenomena are real

### Bridge item 2: official baseline protocol

Problem:
the repo has many reports and modes, but a competition jury needs one official baseline.

Action:

- define the canonical world settings
- define the canonical body or comparison set
- define the official primary metrics
- define the minimum replicate count

Why it matters:

- strong science requires one reference configuration everyone can point to

### Bridge item 3: population sustainability experiment

Problem:
the key scientific transition still fails because stable mortal populations are not yet demonstrated.

Action:

- test the coupled redesign around:
  - home fidelity / clustering
  - continuous reproduction
  - mortality desynchronization
  - population-linked food scaling

Why it matters:

- this is the gate between a rich substrate and real evolutionary dynamics

### Bridge item 4: endogenous evolution evidence

Problem:
inheritance exists in code, but the flagship research claim still needs cross-generation evidence.

Action:

- compare no-mutation, trait-only, morphology-only, and combined-mutation regimes
- measure lineage persistence and diversity over generations

Why it matters:

- this is the most direct route to an `ISEF`-level core result

## 4. Two viable ISEF endpoints

### Endpoint A: successful transition

Best case:

- a stable mortal regime is found
- inherited variation produces measurable evolutionary outcomes
- the project can claim a demonstrated transition from adaptive ecological behavior to stable endogenous evolution

### Endpoint B: principled barrier thesis

If stability still fails:

- prove which structural conditions block the transition
- show why naive tuning fails
- provide a stronger scientific explanation than “it did not work”

This is still a credible `ISEF` path if the evidence is rigorous enough.

## 5. Milestone checklist

### Before YSC

- finalize thesis
- finalize 2 to 3 strongest results
- prepare judge-safe claim boundaries
- prepare the `YSC -> ISEF` next-step narrative

### After YSC, before ISEF push

- complete RNG audit
- freeze official baseline
- run population-stability redesign matrix
- choose the strongest flagship experiment family

### Before final ISEF packaging

- lock the final research question
- produce final figures and confidence statements
- write the cleanest version of the paper and poster

## 6. Strategic rule

The project should evolve like this:

- `YSC`: prove seriousness
- `post-YSC`: prove rigor
- `ISEF`: prove significance

That progression is realistic and strong.
