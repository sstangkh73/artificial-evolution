# Overlap and Submission Strategy

## Non-overlap rule

The two papers share a simulation platform and the general idea of learning from
realized food outcomes. They must not share a primary research question, headline
result, result table, or conclusion.

| Boundary | Paper 1: Food value | Paper 2: Temporal toxin lure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research question | Can direct experience change food choice without semantic labels? | What fails when one observable food type hides time-varying safety states? |
| Independent variable | Value-memory off versus on | Toxic-state frequency and learner/representation |
| Primary outcome | Low-value seed meals per run | Fraction of agents ranking mixed-state fruit above a safe staple |
| Main control | Paired seed, memory disabled | Constant poison, age-aware optimum, analytic boundary, four learner rules |
| Central claim | Experience-based value memory is causally sufficient in the tested abundant-energy regime | Type-only value representations cannot discriminate hidden age states and can produce a lure |
| Explicitly excluded | Toxin lure, partial observability theorem, survival benefit | General proof of food learning, full-ecology fitness cost, evolved deferral |

## Reuse allowed

- A short common platform description, rewritten for each paper.
- A citation from Paper 2 to Paper 1 establishing that the baseline learner can
  avoid a stationary low-value food.
- Shared public code repository and general data-availability statement.

## Reuse not allowed

- Reusing the Paper-1 learning curve as a headline result in Paper 2.
- Presenting the same memory-on/off table as novel in both papers.
- Reusing paragraphs verbatim beyond unavoidable method names.
- Calling either manuscript evidence for intentional understanding, social
  transmission, open-ended evolution, or human-like cognition.

## Submission order

1. Resolve Paper-1 paired analysis, environment-transfer control, authorship, and
   archival package.
2. Submit Paper 1 or release an advisor-approved preprint with a persistent link.
3. Update Paper 2 to cite that record as prior work.
4. Submit Paper 2 as a Letter. If the editor requests a full Article, add the
   preregistered full-ecology fitness experiment rather than padding the text.

## Contingency

If an editor judges the manuscripts insufficiently distinct, merge them into one
Article with the arc:

`stationary value learning works -> hidden temporal state destroys discrimination -> intermittent safety creates the lure`.
